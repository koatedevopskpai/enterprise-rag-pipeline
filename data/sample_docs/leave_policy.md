# Leave Policy

Employees accrue annual leave monthly and may carry over up to five days into
the next calendar year.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance and
approved by the line manager. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days also
require department head approval.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is uncapped but absences longer than three days require a medical
note.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is granted according to local statutory minimums plus the
company top-up described in the benefits handbook.
