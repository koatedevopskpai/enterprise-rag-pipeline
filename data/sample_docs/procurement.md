# Procurement Policy

Purchases must follow the approval thresholds below before any commitment is
made to a supplier.

## Approval thresholds

- Up to 5,000 USD: team manager.
- 5,001 to 50,000 USD: department director.
- Above 50,000 USD: finance committee.

## Preferred suppliers

Goods and services should be purchased from preferred suppliers where a suitable
option exists. A new supplier requires a security and compliance review.

## Contract renewals

Contracts with automatic renewal must be reviewed at least 60 days before the
renewal date to allow time to renegotiate or cancel.
