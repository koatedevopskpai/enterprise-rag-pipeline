# Support Service Level Agreement

Support responds to incidents according to severity.

## Severity levels

- Sev 1: complete outage of a production service. Response within 15 minutes.
- Sev 2: major degradation with no workaround. Response within 1 hour.
- Sev 3: minor issue with a workaround. Response within 1 business day.
- Sev 4: question or request. Response within 3 business days.

## Escalation

If a Sev 1 incident is not acknowledged within 15 minutes, it is automatically
escalated to the on-call manager.

## Post-incident review

A post-incident review is required for every Sev 1 and Sev 2 incident and must
be completed within 5 business days.
