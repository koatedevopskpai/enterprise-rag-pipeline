# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office allowance. Additional
equipment requires manager approval and follows the procurement thresholds.

## Security when remote

Remote workers must use the corporate VPN and enable multi-factor
authentication on all accounts. Confidential data must never be stored on
personal devices.

## Availability

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours and keep their calendar
up to date.
