# Expense Policy

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses that are directly related to
their work.

## Submission window

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date. Claims
older than 60 days require manager and finance approval.

## Limits

| Category | Daily limit | Receipt required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meals | 50 USD | Over 25 USD |
| Local travel | 75 USD | Over 25 USD |
| Accommodation | 250 USD | Always |

## Non-reimbursable items

- Alcoholic beverages unless part of an approved client dinner.
- Personal entertainment and minibar charges.
- Traffic fines and parking penalties.
