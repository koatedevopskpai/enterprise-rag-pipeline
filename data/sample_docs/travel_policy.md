# Travel Policy

Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14
days in advance where practical.

## Flight class

Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Premium economy is
permitted for flights longer than six hours. Business class requires director
approval.

## Accommodation

Hotels should be booked at or below the city rate cap. The cap is reviewed each
quarter by the finance team.

## Ground transport

Use public transport or ride-share for local travel. Rental cars require prior
manager approval and must be compact or mid-size unless a larger vehicle is
justified for equipment.
