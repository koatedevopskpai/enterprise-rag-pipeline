# Data Retention Policy

Records are retained according to their classification and legal requirements.

## Retention periods

| Data type | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Financial records | 7 years |
| Customer support tickets | 3 years |
| Application logs | 90 days |
| Marketing consent records | Until consent is withdrawn |

## Deletion requests

Data subject deletion requests must be fulfilled within 30 days. Deletion is
performed across primary storage and backups within the next backup cycle.

## Legal hold

Records under legal hold are exempt from deletion until the hold is released by
the legal team.
