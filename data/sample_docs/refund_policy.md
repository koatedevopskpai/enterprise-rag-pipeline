# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for most products.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Exceptions

The following items are not eligible for refund:

- Digital licenses that have already been activated.
- Custom or made-to-order hardware.
- Services that have been fully delivered and accepted.
- Gift cards and prepaid credits.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund may be granted when a product is returned with missing
accessories or visible wear. The deduction is calculated from the resale value
of the returned item.
